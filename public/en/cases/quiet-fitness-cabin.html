Product–Service System / Fitness Experience

Quiet
Fitness Cabin

A fitness product–service system concept connecting booking, partner matching, training spaces, modular equipment and post-workout support.

Concept project · 2024–2026Not builtEngineering not validatedOperations and commercial model not validated

02 / RESEARCH

Uncertainty across the conventional fitness experience

Original venue observations, problem notes and journey material point to connected frictions before, during and after a workout.

01

Crowding and high footfall

Density in a shared venue changes expectations about entering and using the space.

02

Feeling watched or exposed

Visibility in an unfamiliar environment can amplify the pressure of taking the first step.

03

Unfamiliar equipment

Complex equipment, uncertain technique and reluctance to ask for help in public create friction.

04

Training alone

A lack of companionship can make both starting and sustaining a routine harder to arrange.

05

Reluctance to ask for help

People may need guidance without feeling comfortable approaching a stranger.

06

Limited place and time flexibility

Opening hours, location and booking status shape whether arrival feels manageable.

03 / FROM PROBLEM TO SYSTEM

Online communication, support arrangements, arrival, space use and post-workout services need to work together.

07 / TESTING, EVIDENCE BOUNDARIES & REFLECTION

Low-fidelity testing surfaced problems; it did not prove that the system works.

The available material supports formative findings and design changes, not validated claims about usability, satisfaction or reduced pressure.

RECORDED

Low-fidelity testing took place, and the material notes that users needed time to understand matching and booking.

ISSUES

The match / booking entry, information transparency, and the visibility of type and icons needed improvement.

CHANGES

The project records a direction of consolidating booking entry points, clarifying step relationships and enlarging text and icons.

COMPLETED

Concept outputs across space, equipment, service and app flows.

DESIGNED, NOT VALIDATED

Matching mechanism, booking experience and service coordination.

CONCEPTUAL

Business model, operating process and venue deployment.

LIMITATIONS

Research and testing evidence is incomplete.

NEXT VALIDATION

Focus on the core “set need → match → book → first workout” journey.

An inclusive fitness experience is not only about entering a space. It is also about controlling how visible, guided and socially involved a person wants to be.
