Overview

Reimagining a neighbourhood bakery café as a warm third place.

Baked Together Café is a self-initiated product-service and spatial experience concept that combines café space design, mobile ordering, workshop booking, loyalty moments and community activities.

The project is clearly framed as a concept portfolio project, not a real client or operating café.

Scope

Service design, spatial experience, brand experience and UX design.

Users

Urban workers, students, young families and neighbourhood residents.

Output

Café concept, mobile touchpoints, service blueprint and business canvas.

Focus

Belonging, slow rituals, food learning and warm community service.

Problem

Many cafés sell food, but fewer create meaningful connection.

Urban café experiences can feel fast, fragmented and transactional.

People may buy a drink, sit alone, and leave without forming any relationship with the place or people around them. The opportunity was to explore how a bakery café could support comfort, learning and participation without forcing social interaction.

01

Rushed atmosphere

Users treat cafés as quick consumption spaces rather than places to slow down.

02

Weak belonging

People want local connection, but joining community activities can feel awkward.

03

Disconnected services

Ordering, loyalty, workshops and in-store experience often feel separate.

Research Insights

The opportunity was not only better food. It was better rituals.

Insights were framed around behaviour, emotion and participation: how everyday eating could become a softer social ritual.

01

Food can become a social trigger.

Shared baking, tasting and seasonal menus create natural conversation moments.

02

Small activities reduce social pressure.

Low-commitment workshops help users participate without feeling exposed.

03

Digital should support warmth.

Ordering and booking should make the service smoother while keeping the café human.

Design Concept

Baked Together Moments

The concept connects choosing bread, joining a table, learning a recipe, collecting seasonal cards and returning to familiar faces.

01

Honest craft

Make the baking process visible and tangible.

02

Warm connection

Create low-pressure opportunities for interaction.

03

Thoughtful digital

Use mobile touchpoints to support the physical service, not replace it.

Spatial Experience

A café space organised around craft, comfort and community.

Spatial zones

Bakery DisplaySupports browsing, sensory attraction and product discovery. Coffee BarMain service point for drink ordering and interaction. Communal TableLow-pressure social area for shared moments. Workshop TableLearning and community activity zone. Pick-up CounterFast service point for pre-orders. Quiet SeatingSlower stay zone for working, resting or eating alone.

The spatial logic supports both quick daily visits and longer community moments, with clear separation between service, browsing, workshop and slow-stay zones.

Business Canvas

A simplified operating model for a community bakery service.

Key partners

Local farmers, flour mills, delivery partners and community groups.

Key activities

Baking, workshops, café operations, digital content and loyalty management.

Value propositions

Fresh food, warm third place, beginner-friendly workshops and app-supported service.

Customer relationships

Personal welcome, loyalty programme, community events and after-visit updates.

Customer segments

Office workers, students, families, hobby bakers and local residents.

Key resources

Café space, bakery team, app touchpoints, workshop tools, recipes and local community knowledge.

Channels

Café storefront, mobile app, local posters, social media and community referrals.

Cost structure

Ingredients, labour, rent, utilities, app maintenance, workshop materials and local marketing.

Revenue streams

Food and drink sales, pre-orders, workshops, seasonal products, loyalty offers and community activities.

Outcome / Reflection

Designing a café as a service system, not just a place to buy food.

This project helped explore how spatial design, digital touchpoints and service rituals can work together to support everyday belonging. The café is framed as a neighbourhood service system where food, learning and small social moments create emotional value.

Clearer service flow

Ordering, workshop booking and loyalty work as one experience.

Stronger community value

Small activities help users participate without social pressure.

Spatial + digital consistency

The café environment and app support the same warm logic.

Next iteration

Test event demand, pickup capacity and how users interpret membership rewards.
